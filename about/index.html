About Project2Pixel

Where Operational Experience Meets Modern Digital Implementation.

Project2Pixel is a founder-led digital transformation and operational systems business helping organisations improve efficiency, modernise systems and build practical digital infrastructure.

Our purpose

Helping Organisations Work Smarter

Project2Pixel exists to help organisations bridge the gap between traditional operations and modern digital implementation.

We believe technology should solve real operational problems. That means looking beyond a website or a single tool and considering how people, processes, communication and digital systems work together.

The Project2Pixel difference

Not Just Digital. Operational.

Operational Thinking

Our approach starts with how the organisation actually works, where friction exists and what needs to improve.

Digital Capability

We bring web development, digital infrastructure, automation, AI-assisted productivity and digital communication capability together.

Practical Implementation

We prioritise solutions that are useful, maintainable and appropriate to the organisation's resources and goals.

Founder-Led Delivery

Clients get direct, accountable communication and a hands-on approach throughout the engagement.

Founder

Meet Natasha Fredericks

Project2Pixel is founder-led by Natasha Fredericks, whose experience spans administration, executive support, project coordination, stakeholder communication, production coordination, website development, social media support, digital tools and practical automation.

This combination of operational experience and modern digital capability shapes how Project2Pixel approaches client work: understand the need, simplify the process, select the right tools and implement a solution that can actually be used.

Natasha's involvement as a Microsoft Elevate Her Ambassador also reflects her commitment to accessible digital skills and practical technology adoption.
