About Project2Pixel

Project2Pixel is a boutique digital support agency helping small businesses and nonprofit organisations build stronger websites, smoother systems and more organised operations.

Why Project2Pixel Exists

Project2Pixel exists because many small teams have strong ideas but scattered digital foundations. We help organise the practical pieces: websites, enquiry paths, booking tools, admin workflows and automation where it genuinely helps.

Founder Story

Project2Pixel was founded by Natasha Fredericks, whose background includes administration, project coordination, digital tools, websites and business support. Her experience across scheduling, stakeholder communication, logistics and operational environments shaped the agency’s practical approach.

What Makes the Studio Different

The difference is the combination of operational and technical experience. Project2Pixel does not only design pages; we think about how people enquire, how information moves, how follow-up happens and how small teams can maintain what is built.

Who We Support

We support small businesses, startups, entrepreneurs, nonprofit organisations, community organisations and professionals building credible personal brands. The studio is based in South Africa and supports clients remotely.
