About Project2Pixel

Project2Pixel is a founder-led boutique digital agency helping small businesses and nonprofit organisations improve their websites, administration, content and digital systems.

Why Project2Pixel Exists

Project2Pixel was created to help businesses and organisations access professional digital support without large-agency costs or unnecessary technical complexity.

Founder Story

Project2Pixel is founder-led by Natasha Fredericks. Her background includes more than ten years of administration, executive support, project coordination, stakeholder communication, production coordination, website development, social media support, digital tools and practical automation.

Natasha’s Microsoft Elevate Her Ambassador involvement also reflects her interest in practical digital skills and accessible technology support.

What Makes the Studio Different

We combine operational experience with modern digital skills. That means we do not only design pages; we also think about enquiries, follow-up, content, documents and systems that small teams can realistically maintain.

Who We Support

We support small businesses, entrepreneurs, startups, NGOs and nonprofit organisations, community organisations, professionals and personal brands.
