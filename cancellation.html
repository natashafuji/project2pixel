Cancellation Policy

How cancellations, refunds, rescheduling and project pauses are handled.

1. Project Deposits

A deposit may be required before work begins to secure project time, planning, research and initial setup.

2. Cancellation Before Work Begins

If a client cancels before work starts, a partial refund may be considered, excluding administration, consultation, planning or third-party costs already incurred.

3. Cancellation After Work Starts

If a client cancels after work has started, the deposit is non-refundable. Completed work and time already spent may still be billed.

4. Monthly Support

Monthly services require written cancellation notice at least 20 business days before the next billing cycle unless otherwise agreed in writing.

5. Client Delays

Delays in providing content, feedback, approvals or access may affect delivery dates. If a project is delayed for more than 14 days due to lack of client response, Project2Pixel may pause the project until required information is received.

6. Third-Party Costs

Third-party costs such as domains, hosting, paid tools, software subscriptions, stock assets or advertising spend are non-refundable.

7. Refunds

Refunds are not provided for completed work, delivered digital services, consultation time, approved project stages or work already in progress.

8. Rescheduling

Clients may request to reschedule consultations or project meetings with reasonable notice. Repeated missed or postponed meetings may affect project timelines.

9. Custom Projects

Custom automation, AI-supported integration, training and reporting work may have cancellation terms specified in the applicable quotation or service agreement.

10. Legal Compliance

This policy is intended to align with applicable South African consumer protection principles and the specific agreement governing the engagement.

11. Contact

For cancellation or refund queries, contact info@project2pixel.co.za.
