Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know exactly what service I need?

No. Tell us what is not working or what you want to achieve. We can help identify the appropriate solution.

Do you work with NGOs?

Yes. Project2Pixel supports NGOs and community organisations with digital infrastructure, communication, operational systems and practical implementation.

Can you work with an existing system?

Where appropriate, yes. We can assess existing digital tools and workflows and identify improvements rather than replacing everything unnecessarily.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Ongoing support can be scoped according to the organisation's requirements and project capacity.
