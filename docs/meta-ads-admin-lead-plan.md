# Project2Pixel — Meta Lead Generation Launch Plan
Date: 16 November 2026

## Priorities
1. Executive & Business Support: paid media priority. 20 active monthly retainer clients is the strategic target.
2. CRM & Workflow Automation: next campaign only after admin campaign tracking and conversion quality are understood.
3. Website Development: final paid campaign; retain published prices.

## Source of truth: published website pricing
- Business Support Lite: R4,500/month, up to approximately 12 hours.
- Executive Support: R7,500/month, up to approximately 25 hours.
- Operations Support: R11,500/month, up to approximately 40 hours.
- Extra support: R400/hour, pre-approved and subject to availability.
- Website subscription: from R300/month, setup from R1,500; larger subscription from R550/month, setup from R2,500.
- CRM/automation: tailored quotation; do not advertise invented fixed package amounts.
- Monthly admin agreements, payment and unused hours must follow the business-support page and contractual terms.

## Launch campaign (not published)
Platform: Meta Ads Manager; campaign objective: Leads.
First test: Admin & Operations only, Facebook and Instagram.
Conversion location: Instant forms; select Higher Intent and use a review screen.
Budget test: R200–R300 per day for 15 days (R3,000–R4,500); only spend after tracking and response capacity are tested.
Audience: begin with South African business decision-makers; test Gauteng first and expand if lead volume/quality warrant it. Use geo and business-relevant interests as hypotheses, not guarantees about job titles or seniority; Meta targeting availability changes.
Placements: Advantage+ placements, review lead quality by placement.
Ads: two distinctive creative angles per ad set; avoid overstating savings and claims.
Offer: Free Business Support Assessment, not unlimited complimentary consulting.
Suggested call to action: Get Quote / Learn More as supported by Ads Manager.

## Meta Higher Intent Instant Form
Headline: Request your free business support assessment
Intro: Tell us a little about your business. We'll contact you about your support needs and available monthly packages.
Fields: Full name, business email, company, phone (optional).
Qualifying questions:
1. Which support do you need? (Executive / Inbox & scheduling / Client follow-ups / Reporting & documents / Project admin / Not sure)
2. Which monthly package suits your budget? (R4,500 / R7,500 / R11,500 / Need guidance)
3. When do you need to begin? (Now / 30 days / 1–3 months / Exploring)
Privacy Policy URL: https://www.project2pixel.co.za/privacy.html
Explain specific enquiry follow-up purpose; optional and separate permission for promotional contact; no bundled mandatory marketing consent.
Thank-you step: We'll review your enquiry and contact you. Link to https://www.project2pixel.co.za/business-support/ or booking URL https://calendly.com/project2pixel-info/30min.

## Shared CRM mapping
Google Sheets: existing Project2Pixel Outreach Leads Tracker, Inbound Leads tab.
Map Meta form name → funnel = Admin & Operations.
Record: received timestamp, name, company, email, optional phone, requested support, timeline, budget, lead source = Meta Lead Ads, campaign name/ID, marketing permission, status = Responded – Interested, next follow-up date.
Do not treat marketing opt-in and consent to reply to an enquiry as the same thing.
Use an approved Meta Lead Ads to Google Sheets connector through Make or Zapier; verify permissions and data retention. Until connected, manually export prospects and import them securely.
Before running ads: submit a test lead, verify receipt, routing, follow-up and cancellation/opt-out handling.

## Operational workflow
New lead → automatic/fast acknowledgement → manually qualify → schedule consultation → tailored proposal → signed contract → invoice in advance → onboarding → monthly renewal.
Operational target: respond during working hours promptly and within one business day.
Track cost per lead AND cost per qualified lead, meetings booked, proposal rate, client acquisition cost, monthly recurring revenue and churn.
After 7 and 15 days: review lead quality, creative, actual cost, bookings and packages selected; pause underperformers. Do not assume a specific CPL or number of wins.

## Phase 2 and 3
CRM campaign: assessment offer, custom quotation, separate form and tracking.
Websites campaign: subscription offer and exact setup/monthly published rates; test instant form against website enquiry when tracking is functioning.
