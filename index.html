Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the size, features and support needed. Starter website and launch setups begin from the packages listed on the services page, with custom quotes for larger builds.

How long does a website take?

A simple landing page can often be prepared faster than a multi-page website. Timelines are confirmed after the discovery call once content, features and scope are clear.

Do you work with NGOs?

Yes. Project2Pixel supports NGOs and community initiatives with websites, forms, reporting structures, donor communication and digital workflows.

Can you help with social media?

Yes. Support can include content planning, post structure, caption guidance and digital communication systems that keep your brand consistent.

Can you set up business systems and automation?

Yes. We can set up enquiry flows, Airtable-style tracking, booking links, email follow-ups, CRM pipelines and practical automation workflows.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Project2Pixel is based in South Africa and supports clients remotely.
